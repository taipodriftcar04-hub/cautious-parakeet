How it works

  1. Create your account

    Choose a username and a password. It takes a minute and needs no email address.

  2. Share pictures

    Post pictures for other members to see. A person looks at every picture before it appears.

  3. Message members

    Send private messages to other members. Install Orbweave on your home screen to open it like an app.

Our promises

Messages and pictures are kept on our hosting provider's servers and are not end-to-end encrypted. The site owner and the hosting provider could technically reach stored data. Within that limit, this is what we commit to.

  • No ads and no selling dataWe run no ads and share nothing with advertisers.
  • No tracking cookiesWe count visits with privacy-friendly analytics that do not follow you around the web.
  • Pictures are cleaned firstLocation and camera details are removed from every picture in your browser before it uploads.
  • You can leave any timeAsk and we delete your account, your pictures and your messages.
